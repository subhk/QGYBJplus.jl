YBJ⁺ wave model

The Young–Ben Jelloul framework evolves a complex near-inertial-wave envelope on the slow balanced-flow timescale.

Wave equation

QGYBJ+.jl advances B according to

\[\partial_t B + J(\psi,B) = -\frac{i}{2}\zeta B -\frac{i f_0}{2}\nabla_h^2 A + \mathcal D_B,\]

where $\zeta=\nabla_h^2\psi$. The terms represent advection, refraction, dispersion, and configured dissipation.

YBJ⁺ relation

For YBJPlus(),

\[B=L^+A =\partial_z\!\left(\frac{f_0^2}{N^2}\partial_z A\right) +\frac14\nabla_h^2A.\]

At horizontal wavenumber $k_h$ this is

\[\widehat B =\partial_z\!\left(\frac{f_0^2}{N^2}\partial_z\widehat A\right) -\frac{k_h^2}{4}\widehat A.\]

The Helmholtz term regularizes short horizontal scales. Recovering A requires one tridiagonal vertical solve per horizontal Fourier mode.

Original YBJ relation

For YBJ(), the Helmholtz term is omitted:

\[B=\partial_z\!\left(\frac{f_0^2}{N^2}\partial_z A\right).\]

The implementation recovers A by vertical integration with coefficient $N^2/f_0^2$ and enforces the tendency solvability condition.

Depth-mean part of the envelope

With $A_z=0$ at the bottom and the lid, $LA$ has zero depth mean, so the wave velocity $e^{-if_0t}LA$ has no barotropic part. In the horizontally uniform column ($k_h=0$) both relations reduce to $B=LA$, so that column of B has zero depth mean; for YBJ() every column does. The model removes any other depth mean from B: from an initial condition (including the depth mean of a SurfaceWave profile, which is small for a surface layer much thinner than the ocean) and at every time step, where refraction would otherwise create it. A depth-uniform envelope is removed entirely, with a warning.

invert_B_to_A!(model)
A = model.fields.A

Wave vertical velocity

The wave velocity is $u+iv=e^{-if_0t}LA$, and continuity gives its vertical velocity (Asselin & Young 2019, equation 2.10),

\[w = -\frac{f_0^2}{N^2}\,\partial_s A_z\,e^{-if_0t} + \text{c.c.}, \qquad \partial_s = \tfrac12(\partial_x - i\partial_y).\]

compute_ybj_vertical_velocity!(model; t) evaluates it where $A_z$ lives, on the faces between levels, with $N^2$ on the same faces. The face values then satisfy discrete continuity with the model's $LA$ exactly, and vanish at the bottom and the lid, where $A_z=0$. As for the QG vertical velocity, the face values are averaged onto cell centres for storage. compute_total_velocities!(model; t) adds it, at the same phase, to the instantaneous velocity.

compute_ybj_vertical_velocity!(model; t=simulation.clock.time)
w = model.fields.w

Select a formulation

plus_model = QGYBJModel(grid=grid, formulation=YBJPlus())
ybj_model = QGYBJModel(grid=grid, formulation=YBJ())
passive_model = QGYBJModel(grid=grid, formulation=PassiveWave())

PassiveWave() retains envelope advection but omits refraction and dispersion. NoDispersion() disables dispersion while retaining the other processes of the selected YBJ formulation.

Initialize and diagnose waves

set!(model; waves=SurfaceWave(amplitude=0.1, scale=30.0))
energy = wave_energy(model)   # domain means: energy.kinetic, .potential, .correction

ETD-RK2 evaluates the explicit wave tendency at both stages and integrates horizontal hyperdiffusion with its exponential factor.
