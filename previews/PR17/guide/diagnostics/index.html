Diagnostics

Diagnostics operate on the model that owns the fields and runtime metadata.

Flow kinetic energy

kinetic_energy = flow_kinetic_energy(model)

The result is reduced across the model communicator.

Wave energy

B_energy, A_energy = wave_energy(model)

The returned tuple reports globally reduced envelope and diagnosed-amplitude energies. The diagnostic reconstructs fields required by the selected wave formulation before reducing them.

Runtime inspection

simulation.clock.time
simulation.clock.iteration
simulation.state
is_root(simulation)
nprocs(simulation)

Use an IterationInterval or TimeInterval when constructing the simulation to schedule energy diagnostics. verbose=true reports progress at the corresponding approximate iteration cadence.

For scheduled component time series, configure EnergyDiagnosticsOutput. See I/O and restart for the files written and examples/compute_energy.jl for spatial post-processing.
