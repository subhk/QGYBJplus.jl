Wave–mean interaction

Balanced flow advects and refracts near-inertial waves. Two-way coupling adds the wave contribution to generalized PV.

Choose the coupling

Use caseComponents
prescribed flow acting on wavesFixedFlow(), NoFeedback()
evolving flow acting on wavesEvolvingFlow(), NoWaveFeedback()
two-way couplingEvolvingFlow(), WaveMeanFeedback()

For example:

model = QGYBJModel(
    grid=grid,
    flow=EvolvingFlow(),
    feedback=WaveMeanFeedback(),
    formulation=YBJPlus(),
)

FixedFlow() always prevents balanced-flow evolution, so it also disables wave feedback: combining it with WaveMeanFeedback() warns and runs without feedback. Both no-feedback components omit wave PV from the inversion; NoWaveFeedback() makes the one-way evolving-flow intent explicit.

Wave PV

For two-way coupling,

\[q^w = \frac{i}{2f_0}J(B^*,B) + \frac{1}{4f_0}\nabla_h^2|B|^2.\]

Here YBJPlus() supplies $B=L^+A$ (the XV$^+$ PV), while YBJ() supplies $B=LA$ (the original XV PV).

Each ETD-RK2 stage diagnoses $q^w$ from its stage-local complex wave field. Streamfunction inversion uses $q-q^w$; the prognostic total q is never modified.

Mean and wave velocities

The balanced streamfunction is that of the Lagrangian-mean flow (Xie & Vanneste 2015, §2; Wagner & Young 2016, eq. 3.22): $\nabla^\perp\psi$ already contains the waves' Stokes drift $\mathbf u^S$. The velocity at a fixed point adds the near-inertial oscillation to the Eulerian mean,

\[u + iv = (u^L + iv^L) - (u^S + iv^S) + e^{-if_0t}LA,\]

and its vertical velocity combines the mean $w^L$, $-w^S$, and the wave vertical velocity. Three calls give these fields, each in model.fields.u, v, and w:

compute_velocities!(model)                                     # Lagrangian mean
compute_total_velocities!(model; t)                            # instantaneous, at time t
compute_total_velocities!(model; include_wave_velocity=false)  # Eulerian mean

compute_wave_velocities! instead modifies the velocity already in the fields, adding the wave velocity and subtracting the Stokes drift, so call it only directly after compute_velocities!.

Particles default to phase-averaged trajectories, advected by the Lagrangian mean; wave_motion=:instantaneous follows the inertial oscillation instead (see Particle advection).

Compare coupled and uncoupled runs

Construct separate models with identical geometry and initial conditions but different feedback components. Each model owns independent arrays and runtime resources, so finalize each simulation independently.

flow_energy = flow_kinetic_energy(model)
wave = wave_energy(model)   # (; kinetic, potential, correction)

Both are domain means on the same scale. The energy the ideal coupled system conserves (Asselin & Young 2019, eq. 3.7) also holds the balanced flow's potential energy, which EnergyDiagnosticsOutput records with the rest as coupled_energy. In the inviscid continuous system, wave–mean exchange is internal to that budget. Dissipation, timestep, and spatial resolution control the corresponding discrete error.

See Xie & Vanneste (2015) for the generalized-Lagrangian-mean derivation and the QG equations for inversion details.
